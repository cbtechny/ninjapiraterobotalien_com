# Batata: the footage that earns the next click

Goal: show a viewer why one more hand matters. Prioritize authentic decisions and visible consequences over another explanation of buttons.

## Record first: one survival decision

- Use the current portrait bout scene, with no debug overlay, desktop cursor clutter, or personal information.
- Capture at 720×1280 or higher in the game’s native portrait framing. Leave a little time before and after the action so the edit has room to breathe.
- Find a real situation with low health and a meaningful choice: spend 2 Coins to Heal/Defend or press the attack. Show HP, Coins, hand result, the selected action, and its consequence clearly.
- Include the rival’s response or the bout result when it completes the story. Do not manufacture a comeback or imply that a selected action guarantees victory.
- Supply the uncut recording. A 12–20 second website edit is the target, not a requirement to rush the original play.
- Audio is optional; the website clip must make sense silently. Explanatory text can be edited in later without changing the recording.
