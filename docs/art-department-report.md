## Section 2 — Flavor Art Opportunities

These subtle, thematic additions are designed to reinforce the "Sackjack Island" identity (Tropical + Card Battle) without disrupting the existing modular layout or site structure.

### 1. Header & Navigation Accents
- **The "Palm-Suite" Marks**: Small, low-opacity card suite symbols (Spades/Hearts/Clubs/Diamonds) stylized as tropical leaves, placed subtly behind the "Sackjack Island" brand title in the header.
- **Nav Hover Indicators**: A tiny "potato sprout" or "playing card corner" that appears above the active or hovered navigation link.

### 2. Kicker Iconography
- **Contextual Minis**: Replace the emoji in the "Latest Buzz" section (⚡, 🎨, 🧪) with bespoke 48x48 comic-style icons:
    - A lightning-charged playing card for "Tuning".
    - A paintbrush dipped in "Hot Pink" for "Visuals".
    - A test tube with a potato eye inside for "Playtests".

### 3. Section Transitions & Dividers
- **Sandy Drift Dividers**: Replace standard horizontal lines with a "sandy texture" edge or a line of tiny, weathered playing cards half-buried in sand.
- **The "Dealer's Shadow"**: A subtle, semi-transparent silhouette of a potato character (The Dealer) peeking from the side of the container in the "Latest Intel" section.

### 4. Corner & Border Flourishes
- **Weathered Sign Post**: Add a small, 100px-wide "wooden sign post" graphic to the top corner of the "About" page cards, making the panels feel like island wayfinding.
- **Potato Silhouettes**: A tiny, dark silhouette of a potato "observer" facing away from the viewer, placed in the bottom-right corner of the site footer to act as a recurring mascot.

### 5. Interaction Flavor
- **Betting Chip Hover**: Buttons could trigger a tiny, animated "betting chip" that slides out or bounces slightly when hovered, reinforcing the blackjack mechanics.
- **Card-Fan Backgrounds**: A very faint, large-scale watermark of a "fanned out" hand of cards in the background of the main content area (e.g., behind the Roster grid).
