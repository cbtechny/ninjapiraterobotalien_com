# 02 - Homepage Blueprint: Sackjack Island Hero

This document defines the layout, content sections, and call-to-actions for the new homepage (HOME).

## Purpose

The homepage is the center of the "digital press kit." Its goal is to captivate visitors with the vibrant energy of **Sackjack Island** and the unique identity of Ninja Pirate Robot Alien Games.

## Homepage Sections (Top-to-Bottom)

1.  **Hero Section**: High-impact "hero" shot of Sackjack Island. Large, dynamic typography, vibrant colors, and a clear call-to-action (CTA) to view the trailer or demo.
2.  **Sackjack Island Showcase**:
    -   Key Features (Bullets or icons with descriptions).
    -   Gameplay Screenshots/Videos (Placeholder imagery allowed initially).
    -   "Play Demo" button (Visible and prominent).
3.  **About Sackjack**: A brief narrative/lore snippet to hook the reader.
4.  **Latest Updates**: A "Latest from the Devlog" section highlighting the three most recent blog posts.
5.  **About the Studio (Condensed)**: A quick overview of the Ninja Pirate Robot Alien team and our mission.
6.  **Contact/Newsletter (CTA)**: A final call to action to follow development or reach out.

## Content Requirements

-   **Hero Image**: Placeholder available (e.g., `https://placehold.co/1200x600`).
-   **Main Heading**: "Enter Sackjack Island: A Vibrant New World."
-   **Core CTA**: "Explore the Demo" (Link to `sackjack-island/demo.html` or similar).

## Interaction Design

-   **Scroll Reveal**: Simple animations for sections as they come into view.
-   **Comic-Book Styling**: Bold borders and shadows for all images and containers.
-   **Vibrant Gradients**: Backgrounds should use dynamic, high-energy gradients that reflect the game's aesthetic.
