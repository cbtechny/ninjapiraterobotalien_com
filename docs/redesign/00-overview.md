# Redesign Overview: Ninja Pirate Robot Alien

This document outlines the overarching vision and goals for the redesign of the official website for Ninja Pirate Robot Alien Games.

## Redesign Goal

The primary goal of this redesign is to consolidate the current multi-page site into a focused, three-page "digital press kit" that centers around our flagship game, **Sackjack Island**. We want a leaner, more impactful site that communicates our studio's identity and flagship product at a glance.

## Digital Press Kit Framing

Rather than a broad studio site, this redesign should feel like a high-energy "press kit" for Sackjack Island. It should provide all the information a potential player, journalist, or partner needs in a concise, visually striking format.

## Visual Direction

-   **Aesthetic**: Vibrant, modular, comic-book inspired.
-   **Tone**: Explosive energy, "Ninja Pirate Robot Alien" (NPRA) attitude.
-   **Execution**: Bold gradients, thick lines, dynamic typography, and punchy calls to action.

## Target Three-Page Structure

1.  **HOME**: Sackjack Island focused landing page.
2.  **DEVLOG**: Chronological devlog/blog.
3.  **ABOUT**: Studio mission, contact, and team information.

## Implementation Constraints

-   **Static-Site Friendly**: Must remain a static HTML/CSS/JS site. No server-side logic (e.g., PHP, databases).
-   **Placeholder Imagery**: Using placeholder images (e.g., from `https://placehold.co/`) is acceptable for planning and early development.
-   **Sackjack Focus**: Sackjack Island must be the hero of the homepage.
-   **Avoid Overengineering**: Keep the codebase clean, readable, and performant. Focus on CSS/HTML/JS simplicity over complex frameworks.

## High-Level Non-Goals

-   Developing a custom CMS.
-   Adding a store or e-commerce functionality (out of scope for this phase).
-   Creating individual landing pages for future (hypothetical) games beyond Sackjack Island.
