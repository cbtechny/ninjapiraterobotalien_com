# 03 - Secondary Pages: Devlog & About

This document details the content and layout requirements for the two secondary pages of the new three-page structure: **DEVLOG** and **ABOUT**.

## DEVLOG (Blog/Updates)

The Devlog page is a chronological record of our development journey with Sackjack Island.

-   **Purpose**: To show that Ninja Pirate Robot Alien is active, evolving, and growing.
-   **Content Requirements**:
    -   A list of devlog posts (most recent at the top).
    -   Each post should include a date, category, title, and a brief excerpt.
    -   Pagination or "Load More" button for older posts.
-   **Layout**: Simple, readable blog format with bold headings and consistent spacing. Use comic-book borders for post thumbnails.

## ABOUT (Studio & Contact)

The About page is the "who we are" and "how to reach us" destination.

-   **Purpose**: To humanize the studio and provide clear channels for communication.
-   **Content Requirements**:
    -   **Studio Mission**: Our "Ninja Pirate Robot Alien" philosophy.
    -   **The Team**: Brief bios and roles (placeholder imagery allowed).
    -   **Contact Section**: Direct links to social media, email, and discord (if applicable).
    -   **Press Kit Link**: A button to download a more formal press kit.
-   **Layout**: Clean and informative. Use a grid for the team section and a clear form or set of links for contact.

## Navigation and Consistency

-   Both pages must share the same header and footer components as the homepage.
-   Consistency in typography and color palette must be maintained across all three pages.
