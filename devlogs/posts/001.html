Welcome to Ninja Pirate Robot Alien

Happy New Year! Today marks the official launch of Ninja Pirate Robot Alien and the beginning of an exciting journey into indie game development. This first devlog covers our mission, development philosophy, and introduces our flagship project: Batata! Spud Royale.

Our Mission

Ninja Pirate Robot Alien was founded with a simple goal: create games that are as fun to develop as they are to play. We believe in transparent development, community engagement, and bold creative choices that push boundaries while staying true to our vision.

Our games blend vibrant comic-book aesthetics with modern game design principles, resulting in experiences that are visually striking, mechanically sound, and narratively engaging.

Our Philosophy

We embrace bold aesthetics with vibrant colors and explosive visual effects. Our development process emphasizes modular design for flexible, expandable systems. We're committed to:

  • Bold Aesthetics: Comic-book inspired visuals with explosive effects
  • Modular Design: Flexible, expandable game systems for rapid prototyping
  • Transparent Development: Sharing our process through regular devlogs
  • Player-First: Gameplay feel and player experience are paramount

Introducing Batata! Spud Royale

Batata! Spud Royale is our flagship project—a vibrant blackjack-style tournament game where potatoes battle it out on a mysterious island. Face off against quirky opponents and make Hit/Stand decisions while automatic Luckys add a twist to each hand.

Your character has a Lucky rank. When that rank lands in their hand, their Lucky triggers automatically. Opening cards and subsequent draws can trigger Luckys; suits do not matter, and opponents have Luckys too.

After your hand resolves, optional actions spend Coins from the current bout: Attack costs 2 Coins to deal 3 damage; Defend costs 2 Coins to gain up to 2 Shield; Heal costs 2 Coins to restore up to 3 HP.

Core Features

  • Blackjack hands with automatic, rank-triggered Luckys
  • Potato characters with distinct Lucky ranks and effects
  • Comic-book inspired art style with bold, vibrant visuals
  • Quick play sessions designed for engaging gameplay

Development Process
