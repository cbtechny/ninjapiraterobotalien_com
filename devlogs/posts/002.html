Building the Island

January 10, 2026 • Devlog #002 • 7 min read

Sackjack now takes place on a single, lively tournament display area. Everything you do happens there: you buy powerups, pick an opponent, and play quick Sackjack bouts. No island wandering, just a focused, fun card tournament that's easy to jump into.

What Playing Feels Like

Matches are short and punchy. You make a few choices, the cards are dealt, and the result is immediate. Opponents are potato characters with clear personalities. Some play safe, some take big risks, so every match feels different. The store sells simple, one-use powerups that give a small edge for the next hand.

How a Session Goes

  1. Enter the tournament display area and see the bracket, the store, and the next opponents.
  2. Buy a powerup if you want. Each one has a short description so you know what it does.
  3. Play a Sackjack bout. It's like blackjack but faster, with extra choices to gamble for a bigger win.
  4. Win to move up the bracket and unlock new fighters, or lose and try again with different powerups.
