How the hand and resource rules work

Coins: reaching 21 earns 2; every other non-bust outcome earns 1, including a loss or a tie. A bust earns none. Lucky bonuses are extra.

Damage: an ordinary loss against the dealer deals 3 damage; a bust deals 4. Shield absorbs damage before HP. Other hand results do not deal this loss damage.

Actions: each costs 2 Coins. After your hand resolves, you may use one available action or skip it. Attack deals 3 damage, Defend adds up to 2 Shield, and Heal restores up to 3 HP.

Limits: HP cannot exceed its maximum; Shield cannot exceed 4. Heal is available when you’re below full health; Defend when Shield is below its limit.

A fresh bout: HP resets to full, Coins and Shield to zero. Between hands in the same bout, your remaining resources carry over.

A familiar hand. A potato twist.

Meet your Lucky

Your character has a Lucky rank. When that rank lands in their hand, their Lucky triggers automatically.

Opening cards and subsequent draws can trigger Luckys. Suits don’t matter, and opponents have Luckys too. A small resource change can help you survive or pay for an action sooner. Drawing another card still risks a bust.

01 / Ace

Draw an Ace → +1 Coin

Ace gets an extra Coin whenever an Ace lands in his hand. That can help pay for an action later.

02 / Pampa

Draw a 6 → restore 1 HP

Pampa’s 6 restores 1 HP when he needs it. Here he’s already full, so the Lucky triggers but adds no health.

Gold is separate from Coins. Gold belongs to your saved profile; Coins pay for actions within a bout.

Beyond a single bout

Eight potatoes. One champion.

Outlast your rival, then take on the next matchup. One lost bout doesn’t end your tournament. A second loss does.
